Company workspace 6

  • Open the Solutions available to your account from one launcher
  • See items that need attention with direct links
  • Switch Solution from the top bar
  • Each user sees only permitted Solutions and data
  • Company logo and colors appear in the workspace
  • Bottom navigation bar on mobile screens

Users & access 6

  • Create a user account linked to an employee
  • Create a client account linked to a client
  • Assign a role to each user
  • Enable or disable a user account
  • Edit the permissions of each role
  • Eight ready roles are created for every company

Approvals 6

  • See requests awaiting your decision, oldest first
  • Approve or reject with an optional note
  • See the workflow steps and the current step
  • See recent decisions
  • Covers leave, loans, employee requests, expenses, invoices and quotations
  • Covers purchase requests, purchase orders and final settlements

Notifications 5

  • Receive an in-app notification when an approval needs action
  • Receive a notification when your request is decided
  • Open the related record from the notification
  • Mark one or all notifications as read
  • Unread count shown in the top bar

Reports 7

  • Choose a date range for all indicators
  • See new and completed work orders and work hours
  • See approved invoice and expense totals
  • See work orders by status and workforce by department
  • See leave by type and by status
  • See passports, visas and documents expiring within 60 days
  • Export work orders to CSV and leave requests to Excel

Guides & support 7

  • Read step-by-step procedure guides by category
  • Search the guides
  • Open a contextual guide panel on each screen
  • Follow a company setup checklist and hide it when done
  • Open a support ticket with category, priority and attachment
  • Reply to a ticket and attach files
  • Follow ticket status

Subscription & billing 10

  • See subscription status, main renewal date and outstanding balance
  • See current Solutions with contract price and renewal date
  • Preview the expected renewal total with tax
  • Browse the Solution catalog with prices
  • Preview price, tax and prorated amount before requesting a Solution
  • Request a Solution with monthly or annual billing
  • Accept a modified offer
  • Schedule cancellation of a Solution at renewal; data is kept
  • See subscription invoices, payments and payment proofs
  • Suggest a custom Solution for review

Account & security 7

  • Sign in with email and password
  • Sign in with a passkey
  • Enable two-factor authentication with recovery codes
  • Add and remove passkeys
  • Reset a forgotten password by email
  • Update name, email and password
  • Choose light, dark or system appearance

Language 2

  • Switch between Arabic and English from the top bar
  • Arabic screens are laid out right to left

Data separation 3

  • Each company's records are separated from other companies
  • Screens open only when the module and permission are active
  • Private files are downloaded only by authorized users

Integrations 2

  • See the status of each integration
  • Open the eInvoicing setup from the integrations page

eInvoicing connector 5

  • See a readiness checklist for eInvoicing
  • Save provider, environment, participant ID and API token
  • Test the connection; it is activated only when the test passes
  • Send an issued invoice to the configured connector
  • See counts of sent, failed and received documents
