Common questions.

How much does H.M One cost?

It depends on the Solutions you choose and the size of your company. Tell us what you need and you get an exact quote before anything starts.

Do I have to buy every Solution?

No. Solutions are bought individually and work on their own. A Solution depends on another one only when the work itself requires it.

Can you build a Solution that is not listed?

Yes. We build custom Solutions on request. Tell us the problem you want to solve and we propose a scope, a timeline and a quote.

Is my company's data separate from other companies?

Yes. Each company has its own isolated workspace, and users only see what their role allows.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. The whole platform works in Arabic and English, including right-to-left layout.
